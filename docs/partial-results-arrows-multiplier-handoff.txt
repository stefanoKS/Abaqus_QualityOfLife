The engineer’s workflow

1. Open a failed one-panel or two-panel run from the usual Results entry. Land paused on the last readable saved field frame, at its actual step and time. Inspect the deformed geometry, fields, contact arrows, closing load–displacement (CLD), door pressure and the failure diagnostic together. Earlier saved frames remain available.
2. Immediately below the white field viewport, with Mesh edges, Node markers and Deformed, find one checkbox for door pressure arrows. Under it choose Automatic (screen size) or Manual (model mm). Manual starts at 5 mm of arrow length for 10 kPa.
3. At the top of the right-hand Results properties, set the dimensionless Result multiplier. Each newly opened result starts at ×1. Edits affect the displayed engineering quantities, their plots, probes, contours, pressure arrow lengths and user-generated curve exports. Solver measurements and model geometry keep their original values.
