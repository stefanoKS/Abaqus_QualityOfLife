What the drawing and live jobs establish

* Modified examples/APLR.dwg SHA-256 d4f4d58e7ef8982b7ff35830dfae8fb6e07c922aa8921373f8af4c0307f20169, 52,493 bytes. Its native inventory is 14 ARC, 10 LINE, 4 LWPOLYLINE, zero SPLINE. All four polylines have zero bulges. Handle 1A3 contains two identical points. Handles 1A4, 1A5 and 1A6 form the seal. The canonical outer ring has 1,764 straight edges; the cavities have 325 and 731. Of 2,820 seal edges, 410 are under 0.01 mm. The shortest is about 0.001 mm and the median is 0.03887 mm. Native filled area 119.5996703 mm². The earlier APLR fixture had a different profile and area.
* The three local operator MESH jobs matching this exact SHA completed in 109.8, 113.2 and 128.1 s. Their model readiness reported mounting.missing and body panel.contact_side_ambiguous, with element 2959 and no first-contact fraction. An isolated run at the same 0.5 mm global size with recombination produced 78,491 seal elements, 180 rigid elements and a successful MESH after 128.4 s. After selecting 51 mounting nodes, Prepare still refused the 10 mm body stroke. Distinguish mesh completion, mounting selection and contact diagnostics in the UI.
* packages/engineering/dwg.py expands each polyline chord into an individual CAD line. packages/engineering/mesh.py::_build makes a distinct OCC point/curve for every chord, so meshing has to accommodate thousands of original vertices. The nominal global size alone cannot eliminate these vertices. packages/engineering/fidelity.py also repeatedly rebuilds node lookups and takes 400 samples even for a straight edge. Profile OCC construction, generation, extraction, fidelity checks and readiness individually before attributing time to a single routine.

Implementation and acceptance gates

1. Keep the exact source contours and entity IDs through import, editing, partitioning and portable files. Build a mesh-only ordered representation for runs of dense linear facets. Simplify across eligible adjacent chords with a deviation budget, including across the closed-loop seam. Preserve real corners, geometric branch points, lip tips, material-region interfaces, cut endpoints, mounting selections, load/contact boundaries, local size controls and necessary FE breakpoints. Steer clear of simple “drop edges below length X” and fixed every-Nth-vertex rules: a short chord can carry a real corner or preserve a thin lip. Keep exact arcs and supported native B-splines in OCC. If a native spline actually needs sampling, evaluate it adaptively over its knot spans under a separate chord-error budget; verify against the original analytic curve.
2. Maintain an explicit mapping from meshing spans to ordered source CAD IDs and arclength intervals. Multiple real edges can be covered by one FE segment. Represent that relationship directly. Avoid claiming a removed source vertex has its own FE node. Where selecting a short CAD entity as a fixture or sizing boundary requires an FE breakpoint, insert it during meshing and explain when remeshing is required. Partition interfaces must be generated once and used by both neighboring material regions with identical node IDs. Keep interface sides out of exterior contact. Include the meshing algorithm/version, tolerance and protected selections in compatibility checks for reuse.
3. Check each candidate against the entire original section. Require the same filled regions, cavity count/nesting, contacts, bonded seams and component identities. Reject self-intersections, closed clearances, inverted or overlapping cells, missing faces and collapsed tagged features. Verify both directions of CAD↔FE deviation, area by region and hole, wall/clearance at nearby opposing segments and the two rigid profiles. Keep the existing 0.02 mm final boundary criterion; allocate only part of that budget to simplification and include the mesher's own error. Tighten locally or retain the original chain if the checks fail. Measure quality and useful through-thickness resolution separately from boundary error. Return a per-region diagnostic when constraints prevent coarsening.
4. Fix the independent Prepare blocker. For the current door down/body up settings, a source-geometry sweep (129 evaluations per native panel arc and the exact exported seal chords) estimated door first touch at 6.27751 mm, body first touch at 10.00857 mm, and minimum body-to-seal separation after 10 mm at about 0.00762 mm. The raw FE assessment also finds no body hit along the prescribed stroke. Its orientation fallback calls this the “predicted first-contact neighborhood” and rejects a nearby body element. Reproduce with and without simplification. Distinguish a verified no-hit/near-hit from an ambiguous side at a real hit. Show initial/final clearance, requested travel, panel and contact uncertainty. Evaluate near-contact using tighter local/reference geometry when the clearance is within the mesh error budget. If the engineer intends noncontact, preserve the requested motion and support an explicit review of the physical rigid face when automatic orientation is indeterminate. If seating is required, explain the missing travel. Preserve ambiguity and penetration blockers where warranted. Never invent contact, silently change the stroke, or flip individual rigid elements to pass a check.
